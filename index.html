Projects

Diffusion Models from Scratch

End-to-end implementation of denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPM) in PyTorch. Covers the forward/reverse diffusion processes, noise scheduling, and U-Net architecture with attention blocks.

PyTorch DDPM Generative AI U-Net

Transformer Language Models

From-scratch PyTorch implementation of the Transformer architecture including multi-head self-attention, positional encoding, and training on language modelling tasks with modern techniques.

PyTorch Transformers NLP Attention

Audio Classification with ConvNets

Urban sound classification using convolutional neural networks on mel-spectrogram features. Trained on the UrbanSound8K dataset achieving strong accuracy on 10 sound categories.

CNNs Audio ML Mel Spectrograms UrbanSound8K

LexRank Text Summarizer

Graph-based extractive summarisation using the LexRank algorithm. Builds a sentence similarity graph with TF-IDF cosine similarity and applies PageRank to identify the most salient sentences.

NLP Graph Algorithms TF-IDF Summarization
